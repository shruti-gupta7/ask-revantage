# Cab & Late-Night Transport Policy

**Document status:** Active sample policy  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective date:** 15 May 2026  
**Owner:** Workplace Operations

## Eligibility

Office transport is available for approved late shifts ending at or after 8:30 PM and for early shifts beginning at or before 6:30 AM. Eligibility depends on the published shift roster.

## Booking a cab

Book by 4:00 PM for the same evening and by 6:00 PM on the previous working day for an early pickup. Add the pickup or drop location, mobile number, cost centre, and manager approval reference.

## Changes and cancellation

Cancel at least two hours before the scheduled pickup. Two avoidable no-shows within 30 days may require manager approval for the next booking.

## Safety

Verify the vehicle number and driver name before boarding. Use the trip-share feature for late-night drops. Report a route or safety concern to Workplace Operations immediately.

## Missed or delayed cab

If the cab is more than 15 minutes late, contact the transport desk using the route card in Ask Revantage. Do not arrange an unapproved replacement unless the transport desk confirms the exception.

## Reimbursement exception

A taxi reimbursement is considered only when the approved cab fails to arrive and the transport desk records the exception. Submit the receipt and reference number within five working days.
