# Workplace & Facilities Guide

**Document status:** Active sample policy  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective date:** 20 February 2026  
**Owner:** Workplace Operations

## Office access

Use your assigned access badge. Report a lost badge immediately and do not lend badges to another person.

## Desk and room booking

Reserve desks and meeting rooms through the workplace booking tool. Release reservations you no longer need so others can use the space.

## Visitors

Register visitors by 3:00 PM on the previous working day. The host must meet the visitor at reception and remain responsible for the visit.

## Workplace issue

Report maintenance, temperature, cleaning, or workspace issues through the facilities request route in Ask Revantage. Add the floor, nearest room or desk number, and a photo when useful.
