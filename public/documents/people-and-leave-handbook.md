# People & Leave Handbook

**Document status:** Active sample policy  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Effective date:** 1 April 2026  
**Owner:** People Services

## Annual leave

Users receive 24 working days of annual leave in each calendar year. Leave should be requested in the people portal and approved by the reporting manager before travel is booked.

## Carry-forward

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried leave should be used by 31 March; unused carried leave expires after that date.

## Sick leave

Users may take up to 10 working days of paid sick leave each calendar year. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive working day.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers may take 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers may take 20 working days within six months of the child joining the family.

## Flexible work

Hybrid work arrangements are agreed with the reporting manager and depend on team coverage, role requirements, and local office guidance.

## Support

Questions that need an individual review should be routed to the People Services contact in Ask Revantage.
