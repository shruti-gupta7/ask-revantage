# Travel & Expense Guide

**Document status:** Active sample policy  
**Version:** 1.8  
**Effective date:** 1 June 2026  
**Owner:** Finance Operations

## Before booking

Create a travel request with business purpose, dates, destination, estimated cost, and the appropriate project or cost centre. Manager approval is required before confirming flights or hotels.

## Approved channels

Book travel through the approved travel portal. Direct bookings require a documented exception from Travel Support.

## Reimbursable expenses

Reasonable ground transport, approved accommodation, business meals within the published limit, visa fees, and required connectivity charges may be reimbursed when supported by itemised receipts.

## Not reimbursable

Personal upgrades, minibar purchases, traffic fines, leisure activities, companion costs, and expenses without a business purpose are not reimbursable.

## Submitting a claim

Submit the expense claim within 10 working days after the trip. Include itemised receipts, the approved travel request, and a brief explanation for any exception.
